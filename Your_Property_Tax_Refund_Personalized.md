## Why Your Refund is Modest

The Minnesota Homestead Credit Refund (Form M1PR) is designed primarily for **lower-to-middle income homeowners**. At your income level:

- The threshold percentage (what you need to pay out-of-pocket) is higher
- The state refunds a smaller percentage of excess property tax
- The program formula is less generous at higher incomes

**However**, your two age 65+ subtractions ($10,400 total) do help by reducing your calculated household income, which improves your refund eligibility somewhat.
